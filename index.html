Mujhe pata hai tumhe ye sab thoda jaldi lag sakta hai, lekin mere liye ye decision achanak nahi hai. Sach kahun toh maine socha tha next month tumhe propose karunga, lekin tumne Instagram deactivate kar diya 😅 Isliye laga ke ab aur delay nahi karna chahiye aur jo dil mein hai, woh keh dena chahiye.

Main tumse timepass ke liye nahi, dil se mohabbat karta hoon. Agar tum haan bolo, toh main is rishte ko nikah tak le jaunga. Main kisi ko jhooti umeed dene wala insaan nahi hoon; jo keh raha hoon, poore dil se aur sach keh raha hoon. Tumhare saath zindagi ke har khoobsurat pal ko jeena chahta hoon. ❤️

Tumhara jawab jo bhi ho, main khushi se qubool karunga. Tum jitna waqt chaho le lena… bas aaj dil ki baat keh di. ❤️
